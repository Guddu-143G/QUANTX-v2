# Factor Specifications

## 1. Value Factor
- **Description**: Identifies undervalued equities relative to their intrinsic worth.
- **Components**: Earnings Yield (E/P), Book-to-Market (B/P), Sales-to-Price (S/P), Dividend Yield.
- **Methodology**: Ranks equities based on the average z-score of the fundamental ratios.

## 2. Quality Factor
- **Description**: Identifies companies with strong profitability and low financial risk.
- **Components**: Return on Equity (ROE), Return on Invested Capital (ROIC), Debt-to-Equity, Operating Margin, Cash Flow Quality.
- **Methodology**: Cross-sectional ranking of aggregated profitability and solvency metrics.

## 3. Momentum Factor
- **Description**: Identifies equities with strong recent risk-adjusted performance.
- **Components**: 6-month return, 12-month return, Volatility-adjusted return.
- **Methodology**: Follows the Nifty200 Momentum methodology by adjusting trailing returns by their standard deviation.

## 4. Low Volatility Factor
- **Description**: Identifies equities with lower historical return variance.
- **Components**: 20-day volatility, 60-day volatility, 120-day volatility, Average True Range (ATR).

## 5. Liquidity Factor
- **Description**: Evaluates tradeability and market depth.
- **Components**: Average Daily Volume (ADV), Turnover, Bid-Ask Spread, Amihud Illiquidity.

## 6. Technical Factor
- **Description**: Captures short-term market dynamics.
- **Components**: Trend, Relative Strength, Breakout signals, Moving Average Crossovers.

## 7. Sentiment Factor
- **Description**: Event-driven NLP scoring of market sentiment.
- **Components**: News entity resolution, Event extraction, Novelty, Persistence.

## 8. Macro Factor
- **Description**: Event-driven macro indicators.
- **Components**: India VIX, USD/INR, 10Y Yield, Crude Oil.
